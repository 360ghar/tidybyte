Clean

Seven rules it never breaks

Approval is its own step.
Selecting a row does not approve it. Every run goes through one confirmation sheet that lists the count for each area.
The Trash comes first.
Files go to the Trash, and Photos items go to Recently Deleted. Empty Trash is the only permanent command, and it asks first.
Each file is checked twice.
Just before a file changes, TidyByte checks its device, inode, size, and modification time again.
Some paths are never touched.
Keychains, certificates and keys, provisioning profiles, .ssh, .gnupg, source code, system paths, Xcode UserData, and simulator devices.
Sizes are honest.
A hard link or an APFS clone that shares its blocks frees nothing alone, and the estimate says so.
Tool cleanups say they are final.
Package-manager and simulator cleanups run the tool’s own command and cannot be undone. Simulator cleanup waits until Simulator and Xcode quit.
Unattended runs never delete.
Automatic maintenance only merges exact duplicates into clones.

What it asks for

Every permission is optional. Without one, TidyByte skips that area and says so.

Permission Why Without it
Full Disk Access Read Mail, Safari, and other apps’ caches and containers. Those areas are skipped, and their sizes show as Unknown.
Photos Find similar, blurry, and large items in the Photos library. Photos & Media covers your folders only.
Notifications Scan reminders, and an offer to clean up after you delete an app. No notifications.

Questions

Is TidyByte for Mac free?

Yes. It is free and open source under the MIT license, with no ads, accounts, or subscriptions.

Is anything sent over the network?

No, unless you turn on an AI provider in Settings, under AI & Privacy. The default is local only. Update checks contact the update feed.

Why is there no button to free up RAM?

macOS manages memory better than an app can. TidyByte shows memory use and pressure in the menu bar, and it does not purge memory.

Why do some sizes say Unknown?

TidyByte could not read that location, usually because Full Disk Access is off. It does not guess.

How do I install and update it?

Open the disk image and drag TidyByte to Applications. The app is signed and notarized by Apple. It updates itself, and Check for Updates in the app menu checks now.

Also on iPhone and iPad.

Swipe through your photo library to keep or delete. Same rule: nothing goes until you say so. The source for both apps is on GitHub.
