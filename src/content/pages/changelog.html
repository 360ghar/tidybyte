v1.0.2 — Storage dashboard redesign

July 2026

Latest Redesigned storage dashboard, cross-tab deep links, and cleanup-tool fixes.

Highlights

  • Redesigned Storage dashboard with a reclaim hero — the biggest wins are surfaced first, with one-tap actions to free them.
  • Cross-tab deep linking: jump straight from a Storage bucket into a swipe session or cleanup tool.
  • Fixes across the cleanup tools (duplicates, screenshots, blurry photos, large files, compression) and a new test suite for the storage dashboard.

Release notes

TidyByte 1.0.2 makes the Storage tab the fastest way to see and reclaim space. The redesigned dashboard ranks cleanup wins by size, and deep links take you straight from a storage bucket into the right tool. This release also hardens the cleanup tools and expands the automated test coverage.

v1.0.1 — iPad support

June 2026

Native iPad support, album picker fix, and settings layout improvements.

Highlights

  • TidyByte is now a universal app — it runs natively on iPhone and iPad, with all orientations supported on iPad.
  • Fixed the album picker dismissing unexpectedly when starting a swipe session.
  • Improved Settings layout on larger screens.

v1.0 — Initial release

January 2026

The first public release of TidyByte on the App Store.

Highlights

  • Swipe-based photo review — keep or delete one card at a time.
  • Duplicate & similar photo detection (SHA-256 + Vision feature prints).
  • Blurry photo detection.
  • Screenshot, burst, and Live Photo cleanup.
  • Large file finder.
  • Video compression.
  • Storage dashboard.

Release notes

Welcome to TidyByte 1.0! Swipe to clean your photo library, find duplicates, blurry shots, and large files, convert Live Photos, and compress videos and photos — all free and 100% on your device.

Notes for reviewers

  • Requires iOS 17 or later. Works on iPhone and iPad (universal app).
  • Photo Library (read & write) is required for the swipe and cleanup tools to function.
  • Notifications are optional and requested only if you enable “Cleanup Reminders.”
  • No account, no in-app purchases, no networking. All processing is on-device.

No older releases — TidyByte 1.0 is the first public build.
