7 Best Free Photo Cleaner Apps for iPhone in 2026

Your iPhone is probably running low on storage, and most of that space is taken up by photos you don't need — duplicates, blurry shots, and old screenshots. We tested every major photo cleaner app on the App Store to find the ones that actually work without charging you a subscription.

How we evaluated these apps

We tested each app against five criteria that matter most when you're trusting an app with your photo library:

  • Privacy — does the app process photos on-device, or does it upload them to external servers?
  • Features — beyond basic duplicate detection, does it find near-duplicates, compress videos, or identify blurry photos?
  • Pricing — is the core functionality free, or locked behind a paywall?
  • User experience — is the interface intuitive, or does it require a tutorial to figure out?
  • Open source — can you inspect the code and verify what the app does with your data?

1. TidyByte

Free Open Source On-Device No Ads

Best overall choice for most users. TidyByte is completely free — no subscriptions, no ads, no upsells. It's also open source, so anyone can verify that it does exactly what it claims.

What sets TidyByte apart is its dual detection system. It uses SHA-256 hashing for exact duplicates and Apple's Vision framework for near-duplicates — photos that look almost identical but differ by a crop, filter, or slight recomposition. Most free apps only catch exact matches.

The swipe-based interface makes reviewing fast — swipe left to delete, swipe right to keep. It also includes video compression, screenshot detection, and blurry photo identification. Everything runs on-device; your photos never leave your phone.

Strengths

  • 100% free with no feature gating
  • Open source — full code transparency
  • SHA-256 + Vision-based duplicate detection
  • Video compression with quality control
  • Intuitive swipe interface for fast review

Weaknesses

  • Newer app with a smaller user base
  • No Android version (iOS only)

2. iOS Built-in Duplicates Album

Free Built-in On-Device

Since iOS 16, the Photos app includes a Duplicates album under Albums → Utilities. It automatically finds and merges exact byte-for-byte duplicates. It's free, it's built-in, and it's perfectly safe.

The main limitation is that it only detects exact duplicates. If you took three nearly identical shots, or edited a photo and saved a copy, the built-in tool won't catch them. It also doesn't offer any review interface — it just merges automatically, which means you can't choose which version to keep.

Strengths

  • Zero setup — already on your iPhone
  • Perfectly safe and private
  • Automatic merging of exact duplicates

Weaknesses

  • Only finds exact duplicates, misses near-duplicates
  • No user control over which version to keep
  • No video compression or other cleanup tools

3. Gemini Photos (by MacPaw)

Freemium Subscription

Gemini Photos has one of the most polished interfaces of any cleaner app. It groups similar photos intelligently and has a satisfying swipe-to-delete flow. MacPaw is a well-known developer with a solid reputation.

The catch is that most features are locked behind a subscription. The free version lets you scan and see results, but actually deleting more than a handful of photos requires a paid plan. If you're willing to pay, it's a good app. If you want full functionality for free, look elsewhere.

Strengths

  • Polished, intuitive interface
  • Good duplicate and similarity detection
  • Reputable developer (MacPaw)

Weaknesses

  • Free version is heavily limited
  • Subscription required for meaningful cleanup
  • Not open source

4. Slidebox

Freemium Ads

Slidebox uses a swipe-based interface similar to TidyByte's, letting you quickly sort photos into albums or delete them. If you like the Tinder-style review approach, Slidebox delivers that experience.

However, the free version includes interstitial ads that interrupt your workflow. It also lacks duplicate detection — it's more of a manual sorting tool than an intelligent cleaner. You'll need to identify duplicates yourself rather than having the app find them for you.

Strengths

  • Swipe-based interface for quick sorting
  • Good for organizing photos into albums

Weaknesses

  • Ads in the free version
  • No duplicate detection
  • No video compression

5. Smart Cleaner

Subscription On-Device

Smart Cleaner offers a broad set of features — duplicate detection, similar photo grouping, contact cleanup, and calendar cleaning. It runs on-device, which is good for privacy.

The problem is the pricing. The app requires a subscription to access most features, and the subscription is on the expensive side compared to what you get. The free tier only lets you scan; actual cleanup is paywalled.

Strengths

  • Broad feature set (photos, contacts, calendar)
  • On-device processing

Weaknesses

  • Expensive subscription
  • Free version is essentially a demo
  • Not open source

6. Remo Duplicate Photos Remover

Freemium

Remo focuses exclusively on duplicate photo detection. It scans your library, groups duplicates, and lets you choose which versions to keep. It does this one job reasonably well.

The limitation is scope. It only handles duplicates — no similar photo detection, no video compression, no blurry photo identification. If you're looking for a comprehensive cleanup tool, this only covers one piece of the puzzle.

Strengths

  • Simple, focused duplicate removal
  • Straightforward interface

Weaknesses

  • Duplicates only — no near-duplicate or similarity detection
  • No video or screenshot cleanup
  • Limited free functionality

7. Phone Cleaner: Clean Storage+

Subscription Cloud Processing

Phone Cleaner: Clean Storage+ offers a wide range of features — duplicate detection, similar photo grouping, large file detection, contact cleanup, and more. On paper, it's one of the most feature-rich options.

The concern is privacy. Some of the app's analysis features rely on cloud processing, meaning your photos may be uploaded to external servers for analysis. The app also requires a subscription for meaningful functionality. If privacy matters to you, this is a significant red flag.

Strengths

  • Comprehensive feature set
  • Includes contact and file cleanup

Weaknesses

  • Cloud processing raises privacy concerns
  • Subscription required
  • Not open source

Frequently asked questions

What is the best free photo cleaner app for iPhone?

TidyByte is the best free photo cleaner app for iPhone. It is completely free with no ads or subscriptions, runs entirely on-device for privacy, and offers both exact duplicate detection (SHA-256) and near-duplicate detection (Apple Vision). It also includes video compression and a swipe-based review interface.

Is it safe to use photo cleaner apps on iPhone?

It depends on the app. Apps that process everything on-device — like TidyByte — are safe because your photos never leave your phone. Be cautious with apps that upload photos to cloud servers for processing, as this creates privacy risks. Always check the app's privacy policy before granting photo library access.

How does Gemini Photos compare to other cleaner apps?

Gemini Photos by MacPaw has a polished interface and good duplicate detection. However, most of its useful features are locked behind a subscription. The free version is very limited. If you want full functionality without paying, TidyByte offers comparable detection quality at no cost.

Do photo cleaner apps delete photos from iCloud?

Yes, if iCloud Photos is enabled, deleting a photo from your library through any app will also remove it from iCloud and all your synced devices. Deleted photos move to the Recently Deleted album where they stay for 30 days before permanent removal. Always review selections carefully before confirming deletion.

How often should I clean my iPhone photo library?

For most users, a monthly cleanup is sufficient. If you take a lot of photos or videos, a quick weekly review can prevent accumulation. Running a duplicate scan monthly and reviewing screenshots and blurry photos every few weeks keeps your library manageable without being tedious.
